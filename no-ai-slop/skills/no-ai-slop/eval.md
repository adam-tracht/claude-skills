# No AI slop eval

Run this in your reasoning before returning anything. It is never printed. The user sees prose, an edited draft, or a findings report, depending on the mode. They do not see this checklist, your pass marks, or your evidence.

**How to run it:**

1. Work through the checks that apply to your mode.
2. On any failure, quote the offending line to yourself and name the fix. An unsupported "pass" is not a pass; if you cannot point at the text, you have not checked.
3. Fix every failure, then rerun the checks you broke.
4. Return the output. Say nothing about this file.

Do not emit a pass list. Do not summarize what you checked. Do not mention that an eval exists.

---

## Universal checks

Apply in every mode.

1. Are banned words, empty adverbs, and filler phrases gone, except where quoted as an example or part of a source quote?
2. Are binary contrasts and negative listings stated directly instead?
3. Are throat-clearing openers, faux-insight setups, and rhetorical question-and-answer pairs cut?
4. Are colon reveals rewritten as plain sentences?
5. Is superficial -ing analysis replaced with the actual consequence?
6. Is importance puffery replaced with the plain fact?
7. Is weasel attribution replaced with a named source, or flagged to the user where no source exists?
8. Is synonym cycling gone, with the right word simply repeated?
9. Are forced tricolons and false ranges cut?
10. Is the fake-profound kicker deleted rather than rewritten into a better metaphor?
11. Is the summary-recap ending gone, so the piece ends on a concrete point, takeaway, or next action?
12. Is formatting slop gone: emoji headings, decorative bold, bold-lead bullets, title-case headings, bullets that should be prose, headers over tiny sections?
13. Are there zero em dashes outside of quoted source text or pattern examples?
14. Does the prose avoid robotic symmetry, repeated sentence shapes, and stacked punchy fragments?
15. Does it use active voice with human subjects, with no inanimate thing performing a human verb?
16. Are the claims concrete, with specific facts and numbers preserved rather than smoothed into generic importance?
17. Would this sound natural read aloud to a sharp colleague?

## Edit mode only

18. Does the edit preserve the user's point without adding claims, examples, stats, quotes, or opinions they did not have?
19. Name the three to five voice signals you identified. Does each one survive in the edited draft?
20. Did you leave strong human sentences alone rather than rewriting them for consistency?
21. Is the amount of cutting proportional to the actual slop, with no compression that strips character?
22. Do clear spoken cadence, fragments, and changes of pace remain intact, with only genuinely tangled sentences rewritten?
23. Does personal setup that adds context, tension, or character survive?
24. Is the writer's structure preserved, unless it was hurting the piece and you said why?
25. Would the writer recognize this as their own voice?
26. Is the output the full edited draft plus a **What changed** section of five bullets or fewer, with no eval output and no scoring?

## Draft mode only

27. Does the draft have a point of view rather than a balanced summary of everything?
28. Does it react to the facts instead of only listing them?
29. Is at least one sentence specific about feeling or judgment rather than abstractly evaluative?
30. Does sentence length actually vary across the piece?
31. Is the output the prose alone, with no What changed section, no checklist, no notes on patterns avoided, and no explanation of your choices?

## Detect mode only

32. Is every pattern named, with the offending line quoted and a fix in a few words?
33. Did you avoid rewriting the draft?
34. Did you avoid scoring the draft on any scale?
35. Did you avoid claiming or guessing whether AI wrote it?
36. Did you offer to edit the draft afterward?
